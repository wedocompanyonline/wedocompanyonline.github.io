01 / Multilingual DTP

Layouts that speak every language.

A translated document is more than its words. Translation keeps the meaning and we keep the look. Flyers, brochures, manuals, presentations and PDFs, formatted page by page in Latin, Cyrillic, CJK and right-to-left scripts, aligned and true to the original. If the source file is gone, we recreate it, faithful to the source, style included.

eLearning,
audio & video

Localizing a course means localizing every layer. Screens, interactions, narration, captions and even the text inside graphics, rebuilt in Storyline, Rise and Captivate and checked against quality-control guidelines to ensure a balanced, harmonious result that matches the source file in its original language.
